abstract: "Cultural heritage digital experiences pose unique evaluation challenges that established UX frameworks inadequately address. This paper proposes a taxonomy for cultural UX evaluation integrating contextual inquiry, mixed-methods approaches, and visitor typology frameworks. We identify evaluation dimensions specific to cultural contexts — epistemic engagement, narrative coherence, affective resonance, and accessibility equity — and validate the taxonomy through a multi-site study across three Greek heritage institutions. The taxonomy provides practitioners and researchers with a structured lens for evaluating digital cultural experiences beyond standard usability metrics."
