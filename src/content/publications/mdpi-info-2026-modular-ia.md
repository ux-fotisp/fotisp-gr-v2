abstract: "Digital cultural heritage systems face a tension between information richness and cognitive accessibility. This paper presents a modular information architecture (MIA) framework enhanced by AI-driven content organisation, designed to surface relevant artefacts and narratives for diverse visitor profiles without overwhelming cognitive load. We describe the framework's design principles, the AI classification layer for multi-profile visitor routing, and an evaluation with 148 visitors across two heritage sites. Results indicate a 31% improvement in navigation task completion and a 24% reduction in time-to-discovery for target artefacts compared to conventional IA approaches."
