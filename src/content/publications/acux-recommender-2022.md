abstract: "This paper presents ACUX Recommender, a mobile recommendation system designed for cultural heritage visitors with heterogeneous visiting preferences. Building on the ACUX Typology visitor classification framework, the system profiles visitors across five archetype dimensions and generates contextually relevant recommendations for artefacts, routes, and narratives within cultural heritage spaces. A prototype evaluation with 62 participants across two Greek museums demonstrated a 78% acceptance rate for recommendations and a significant increase in self-reported discovery satisfaction. The system's classification layer is discussed in relation to its adaptability to different institutional scales."
