abstract: "Existing cultural-visitor typologies — developed independently across museum studies, digital heritage, and tourism research — exhibit significant conceptual overlap and terminological inconsistency, impeding cross-institutional and cross-disciplinary research. This paper proposes the ACUX Typology: a harmonised, multi-profile classification framework that synthesises 14 established typologies into five orthogonal visitor archetypes (Explorer, Scholar, Socialiser, Casual, and Purposive). We describe the derivation methodology, present the typology schema with operational definitions, and validate it through a card-sorting study (N=94) and a diary study across three heritage sites. The ACUX Typology provides a stable foundation for personalised digital heritage systems, evaluation frameworks, and visitor experience research."
