## Trust before the add-to-cart

Organic food buyers are, by definition, information-motivated. They chose a more expensive, less convenient option because they believe something about the product — where it was grown, how it was certified, who made it. The existing site treated that belief as a given rather than something to earn on every product page.

The provenance panel was the highest-ROI design decision in the project. It cost one day of engineering work and moved conversion more than any navigation or checkout change.
