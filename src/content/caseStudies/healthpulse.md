## Measuring without fear

The most revealing moment in this project came from a diary study participant who had completely stopped measuring her blood pressure. Not because she forgot — because she was afraid of what she would see.

That insight reframed the design problem. The question stopped being "how do we make the app more usable" and became "how do we make it safe to know."
